02 / What happens when you visit

Privacy, without the fog.

A straightforward description of this app as currently built—not a promise about every hosting provider.

What we collect

No accounts. No profiles.

FlightWeather does not ask you to create an account, and the app does not intentionally collect your name, email, precise location, or other profile information. Airport searches are sent to our server so it can return airport and weather data.

How requests work

Searches go through the server.

When you search, the server contacts the data providers listed above. Those providers receive requests from the server rather than directly from your browser for the briefing data. The map uses proxied OpenStreetMap tiles; the Leaflet library is loaded from unpkg in your browser.

App storage

Temporary service caches.

The server temporarily caches public weather, aircraft, runway, and map data to reduce repeat requests. The app does not intentionally store a profile or search history in a user account. Hosting providers may keep routine technical logs under their own policies.

Third-party services

Other services have their own rules.

Cloudflare hosts the deployed app, unpkg serves the Leaflet library, and public data services provide the information described above. Their services may process technical information such as IP addresses or request metadata. Review their privacy terms for details.

Questions or requests

About privacy questions.

This hobby project does not currently publish a privacy contact address. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the site.

03 / Cookies & browser storage

No cookie jar in the app.

Current app behavior

We do not set app cookies.

The FlightWeather application does not currently set cookies or use local storage, session storage, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. Your search and selected airport are used to show the current page and are not saved by the app for later visits.

Our hosting, content-delivery, map, or library providers may use or receive their own technical identifiers when serving their services. Their behavior is outside the app’s direct control and may change; consult their current privacy documentation.

04 / A note about the data

Useful context, not flight advice.

Data freshness & safety

Always check official sources.

Data can be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or interpreted incorrectly. FlightWeather is a student hobby project and is for general information only—not for flight planning, dispatch, or operational decisions. Always consult current official aviation sources and qualified professionals.
